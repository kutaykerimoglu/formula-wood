Reading the aero balance

The gauges compare your build with the stock car at the same speed.

Drag · straights

Drag pushes against the car. A steeper wing can add grip, but with the same motor it takes longer to gain speed on a straight.

Downforce · corners

Downforce presses the tyres into the track. Its effect grows with speed, so it matters more through a fast bend than a slow hairpin. Too much wing tilt can stall the wing: drag remains high as load falls away.

Balance · front and rear

Read the axle gauges together. Extra rear load offers little cornering benefit if the front tyres already limit the car. Compare sector times after a change to see where the trade pays off.
